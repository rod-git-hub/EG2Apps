# Changelog

User-visible changes per released version of each app.

## 1.0.1 (beta)

- **Both:** choose how lists look on the glasses: the text cursor or Even's select box (phone page → Glasses lists).
  The app's logo on the glasses and the phone page. Fixed: a rare blank glasses screen when the app starts.
- **EG2Presentation:** with Even's select box, the stopwatch's time shows in the header.

## 1.0.0 (beta)

- **EG2Notes:** first release: your notes and checklists on your G2 glasses, created on your phone. Tick items with a
  tap and read notes a page at a time. Works offline; your notes stay on your phone.
- **EG2Presentation:** first release: your presentations on your G2 glasses, topic by topic, with a talk stopwatch.
  Tap to mark a topic done and see the next. Works offline; everything stays on your phone.
- **Both:** light and dark phone page.
