# EG2Notes (user guide)

Notes with an optional text and an optional checklist. Created and edited on the phone; read, ticked, moved and
deleted on the glasses.

## On the glasses

- **The list:** your notes, `■` done or `□` open, with ticked/total items, e.g. `□ Groceries (1/9)`. Tap one to open
  it. With no notes: "No notes yet. Add one on your phone."
- **A note:** **≡ Note text** (only when it has text), **● Mark note done / open**, a blank line, then its checklist.
  **Tap an item to tick or untick it**: the cursor stays on that item. The header shows the title and ticked/total.
- **Note text:** a page at a time; swipe for the next or previous page; double tap = back.
- **Glasses menu** (tap, then press and hold): on the list or a checklist, Move up, Move down, Select and Delete for
  the row under the cursor; on the note's text page, for the note. Deleting a note deletes its checklist too (it says
  how many items). See [glasses-menu.md](glasses-menu.md).

## On the phone

- **The list:** **New note** → Add. Tap `□` / `■` to mark a note done or open, its title to open it, ▲ ▼ to move it.
- **A note:**
  - **Title** → Save;
  - **Mark note done / open**;
  - **Checklist:** tick `□`, tap an item's text to rename it, ▲ ▼ to move it, ✕ to delete it (tap twice);
    **Add an item**; **Check all**, **Uncheck all**, **Clear ticked** (removes ticked items, tap twice);
  - **Note text** → Save text (shown on the glasses under ≡ Note text);
  - **Delete note** (tap twice).
- **◀ All notes** goes back.

## Your data

Saved on your phone only, in the Even Realities app's plugin storage, a moment after every change. Other plugins can
read that storage: don't keep passwords or secrets in notes. Keep a copy with **Backup → Export**
([settings.md](settings.md)).

Limits: 500 notes, 200 checklist items a note, titles and items up to 200 characters, note text up to 10,000.
