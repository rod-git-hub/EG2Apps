# Privacy policy: EG2Notes and EG2Presentation

**Effective:** 23 October 2026 · **Applies to:** EG2Notes and EG2Presentation, plugins for Even Realities G2 glasses
distributed through Even Hub.

## In short

- The apps have **no internet access**, **no account**, **no ads**, **no analytics** and **no AI**.
- They request **no permissions** (no network, microphone, location, camera or photos).
- Everything you write stays **on your phone**, in the Even Realities app's storage.
- The developer never receives, sees or stores your data. There is no server.

## What the apps store

- **EG2Notes:** your notes (title, text, done or not) and checklist items (text, ticked or not).
- **EG2Presentation:** your folders, presentations and topics (title, details, done or not), and the talk
  stopwatch's time.
- **Both:** your settings: Appearance for the phone page (Match phone, Light or Dark) and Glasses lists (text
  cursor or Even's select box).

Nothing else: no name, email, contacts, location, device identifiers or usage data.

## Where it's stored, and who can see it

- On your phone, in the Even Realities app's plugin storage, through the Even Hub SDK.
- **Other plugins installed in the Even Realities app can read that storage**, and so can the Even Realities app
  itself (it runs the plugin). We tested this on a real phone. So **don't keep passwords or other secrets** in your
  notes or presentations.
- What appears on your glasses travels from your phone to the glasses over Bluetooth, through the Even Realities app.
- Even Realities' own handling of data in its app is covered by Even Realities' privacy policy.

## Your choices

- **Export:** the phone page's Backup section gives you all your data as plain text to keep.
- **Import:** brings an export back (it replaces what's there, after a second tap).
- **Delete:** delete items on the phone or from the glasses menu (every delete asks first). Uninstalling the app
  deletes its data. There is no other copy anywhere, so export first if you want to keep it.

## Children

The apps collect nothing from anyone, children included.

## Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page with a new effective date, and noted in the app's release notes.
